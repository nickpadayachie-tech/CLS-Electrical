    Home/About

    An electrical contractor trusted since 1986

    CLS Electrical was established to provide project management and electrical services. We have extensive experience within our field and are committed to delivering an unsurpassed quality of service and post-commissioning care.

    Who We Are

    Multi-disciplinary. 100% BEE owned. Nationally present.

    CLS Electrical is a multi-disciplinary electrical engineering company based in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and more recently established in Bloemfontein.

    Originally trading as CG Electrical, the organisation has undergone strategic alignment to meet current employment equity and BEE criteria — all key personnel were retained and still form part of the highly professional team.

    We are proud to be 100% BEE compliant.

    What We Do

    Locally owned, expert in all electrical fields

    Electrical Management & Supervision

    Project management of the entire electrical process — planning, organising, controlling and monitoring the schedule to strict quality and safety standards.

    Supply of Material

    Material not supplied by the client, plus a purchasing and inspection service against client procurement or engineer's specification.

    Installation

    The physical erection of MV and LV systems for industrial plants, commercial and residential projects — managing skilled and unskilled labour to plan.

    Calibration & Commissioning

    Sophisticated test and calibration equipment, operated by highly specialist technicians, to ensure optimal operation once erection is complete.

    Maintenance

    Ongoing maintenance to ensure full operational efficiency after completion of the project.

    Turnkey Project Delivery

    We handle projects on a turnkey basis as part of a team involved extensively in electrical projects since 1986.
