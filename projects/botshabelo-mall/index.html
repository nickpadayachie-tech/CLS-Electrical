    Scope

    Complete electrical infrastructure for the Botshabelo Mall development — from substation construction to tenant fit-outs across the full retail centre.

    Electrical work performed

    • Building and commissioning of the substation
    • Construction and installation of 4 × 500KVA mini-substations
    • Complete installation of main distribution boards and connection of 3 × 500KVA generators
    • Internal and external mall lighting and reticulation
    • Tenant fit-outs — Woolworths, Pick & Pay, Shoprite, Checkers, Truworths, Markhams, Foshini, banks, Sportscene, Hungry Lion and more
    • Cashbuild complete fit-out, KFC drive-through and taxi rank fit-out
