    Sandton City — Electrical Installation

    R46.8M
    Client
    WBHO / Standard Bank / CKR Consulting Engineers
    Location
    Sandton City, Sandton, Johannesburg
    Project Value
    R46.8M
    Status
    Completed
    Completion
    Delivered across multiple phases; food court and Liberty 2 Degrees packages completed
    Discipline
    MV & LV Electrical

    Scope

    Five major electrical packages across the Sandton City complex: Level 2 (Hamleys, Milky Lane, Spur, mall area), Level 4/6 (Dischem, Pick & Pay Clothing, Exclusive Books, Superdry), the full food court revamp, and the Liberty 2 Degrees Checkers/tenant fit-out.

    Electrical work performed

    • Complete electrical installation of light DBs, metering, dimmer panels and LED technology
    • HVAC supply and plantroom configuration
    • Removal and replacement of existing wireways, cable baskets, trunking and conduits
    • New DB installation for Checkers area and retail stores; complete LV/MV supply from SUB 7 to all major DBs including 650m of 185×3-core cable from Sub 7/8 to the main transformer room, including earth
    • Food court revamp — remodelling, lighting, HDMI fit-out, LED tech, cove and bulkhead lighting, screen supply and solar application
    • Commissioning and testing of all DBs and metering
